Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

Closa Health gives you a free care plan based on your answers. Your answers are about your health, which is sensitive information, so we keep what we collect to a minimum and tell you plainly what we do with it.

We handle your personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and, where it applies to you, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

Who we are

Closa Health is responsible for your information (the “data controller”). To ask us anything about your data, email privacy@closahealth.com.

What we collect

  • Your answers to the questionnaire. These are health information.
  • Your email address, so we can send you a link to your plan.
  • Your first name or nickname, only if you choose to give one. We use it to personalise your plan and your email.
  • Your answers about pregnancy plans, if you give them. We use them to build your plan, but we don’t store them.
  • Whether you ticked “Keep me updated about new features on Closa Health”. This box is optional and is never ticked for you.
  • Anonymous usage statistics, such as which pages are visited, how far people get through the questions, which device type is used (phone, tablet or computer), which campaign link or website brought the visit, and which buttons are tapped (for example “Find a gynaecologist”). These use a random code that is made for your visit and is not linked to your email, your name or your answers. If your browser’s “Do Not Track” or “Global Privacy Control” setting is on, we don’t record these.
  • A short anonymous summary of each plan, such as an age band, the main concerns and the result (for example how soon the plan said to be seen). It contains no email, name or link back to you.

If you add a period-app file, it is read on your own device. The file is never uploaded. Only the suggested answer is kept.

We don’t use advertising or tracking cookies, and we don’t use third-party advertising trackers. Your browser keeps your progress for a short time so a page refresh doesn’t lose your answers.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To build your care plan, save it, and email you the link. We do this with your consent, which you give by pressing “See my plan” after reading this policy. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your plan.
  • To tell you about new features, if you ticked the box. We do this with your consent. You can stop at any time, and we will remove you from this list if you ask, or if you delete your plan.
  • To understand and improve Closa Health, using anonymous totals (see below), and to keep the site secure and stop misuse. We do this because it is in our legitimate interest, and it does not need your name or your answers.

We never sell your data, and we never use your answers for marketing.

Anonymous statistics

We look at combined totals, for example how many people finish the questionnaire, or how many people say their periods are heavy. We don’t look at individual people in these totals. Groups that are too small to be anonymous are hidden. We don’t use your individual answers for research. If that ever changes, we will ask for your permission first.

Who can see your information

Your plan opens with a long private link in the email we send you. Anyone who has the link can open your plan, so please don’t share it publicly.

Email addresses and names can only be seen by authorised Closa Health team members, behind a separate password. Everyone else, and everything shown in our statistics, sees only anonymous totals.

Our website and email are provided by our hosting company, Hostinger, which stores your information for us and may only use it to provide that service. We don’t share your information with anyone else unless you ask us to, or the law requires it.

Where it is stored

Your information is stored on our hosting provider’s servers, which may be outside Nigeria. Wherever it is stored, we apply the same protections described in this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep your saved plan until you delete it. When you delete it, we remove your email, name and answers. We keep only a note that a plan was deleted, and when, so that our totals stay accurate. Anonymous statistics are not linked to you, so they stay too.

To stop abuse, we briefly keep your internet address, for up to two hours, when you save a plan, use the statistics tracking or sign in to our dashboard. Our hosting provider also keeps standard server records.

How we protect it

Your answers travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). Our database is kept in a private area of the server that cannot be reached from the web, and only we can access it. Our dashboard is protected by passwords, and email addresses need a second one.

Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • show you the information we hold about you
  • correct it
  • delete it
  • stop or limit how we use it, or object to it
  • give you a copy you can take elsewhere

You can delete your saved plan yourself at any time using the “Delete my saved plan” button at the bottom of your plan page. For anything else, to leave the “new features” list, or if you’d like us to delete it for you, email privacy@closahealth.com. We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your information, you can complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. If you are in the European Union or the United Kingdom, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Closa Health is meant for adults. If you are under 18, please ask a parent, guardian or doctor to help you.

Guidance, not diagnosis

Your care plan is guidance based on your answers. It is not a diagnosis. If you need urgent help, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital emergency unit.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we use your information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
