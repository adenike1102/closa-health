Privacy policy

Draft for review. Closa Health must have this checked by a legal adviser (including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023) before launch.

What we collect

The answers you give in the questionnaire, and the email address you give us. If you tick the optional box, we also save your pregnancy plans. If you don’t tick it, we use them to build your plan but we don’t store them.

If you add a period-app file, it is read on your own device. The file is never uploaded. Only the suggested answer is kept.

Why we collect it

To build your care plan, to email you a link so you can come back to it, and to show it to a doctor. We never sell your data, and we never use your answers for marketing.

Who can see it

Your plan is opened with a long private link in the email we send you. Anyone who has the link can open your plan, so please don’t share it publicly. We only share your information with others if you ask us to.

Analytics

We count when people tap buttons such as “Find a gynaecologist”, with the category and city. These counts are not linked to your email or your answers.

Deleting your data

You can delete your saved plan at any time using the “Delete my saved plan” button at the bottom of your plan page. You can also email privacy@closahealth.com and we will do it for you.

Guidance, not diagnosis

Your care plan is guidance based on your answers. It is not a diagnosis. If you need urgent help, call 112 or go to the nearest hospital emergency unit.
